Add BAG to Chrome

Save a page, link, or selection to this BAG from Chrome’s right-click menu.

Download extension
  1. Download the ZIP above and extract it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in desktop Chrome and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json. Keep that folder on your device.
  4. Open To my BAG from Chrome’s extensions menu and sign in with your BAG account.
